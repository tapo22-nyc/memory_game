How to Play
Spelling Bee
🔤 Make words using the 7 letters in the hive. Click letters or type with your keyboard.
⭐ Every word must include the center letter (shown in pink/purple).
🔁 Letters can be reused as many times as you like.
📏 Words must be at least 4 letters long.
🌟 A Pangram uses all 7 letters at least once. Can you find it?
⟳ Use the Shuffle button to rearrange the outer letters.
